About Kenyan Excellence Awards

Kenyan Excellence Awards is a public voting platform built to recognize people making a real impact across Kenya — in entertainment, media, and public life. Anyone can nominate someone they admire, and the public decides who stands out through open, transparent voting.

We believe recognition matters. Whether it's a radio presenter connecting with listeners every morning, an artist shaping the sound of a generation, or a public figure serving their community, we want their work seen and celebrated by the people who follow it.

Every vote is KSh 20, cast securely by M-Pesa, and counted publicly in real time — no hidden tallies. Categories can also be sponsored for a Free Voting Day, opening participation to everyone at no cost. Nominees who reach strong support are recognized with real prizes, up to KSh 500,000 for reaching 7,000 votes in a category.

This platform is new and growing. We're building it openly, category by category, and we invite the public, nominees, and sponsors to be part of shaping what it becomes.
